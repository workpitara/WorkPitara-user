Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do I access task guidelines?

All standard operating procedures (SOPs) are available inside your authorized portal dashboard at Base44 Portal.

Q2: Do I need a laptop or can I work from a mobile phone?

While basic practice and review can be seen on mobile, a desktop or laptop is strongly recommended for standard typing and data entry productivity.

Q3: How are task payouts processed?

Task balances undergo automated accuracy checks and manual validation. Cleared sums are transferred via verified UPI ID or direct NEFT/IMPS bank transfer.
